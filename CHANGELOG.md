# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-27

First release.

- **Loot & merchants**: fast loot, selling grey items (with a keep list), repairing (guild bank optional), Alt + click buys a full stack, DELETE filled in, quality borders on the character window, bind on pickup confirmed when alone.
- **Windows**: drag Blizzard's windows (including the bag windows by their title bar and portrait; right click there opens the bag menu), positions kept for all characters, per character or until logout, Ctrl + mouse wheel scales, Shift + Ctrl + right click resets.
- **Bags**: slots filled from the top left, reagent bag docked below the combined bag; bag bar without its outer frame, in a direction you choose, fading, and an arrow that folds the bag slots and the key ring away.
- **Menu & action bars**: micro menu without its outer frame, fading, single buttons hidden while the others close up in a direction you choose; key binding and macro texts, action bar art, out of range colour. The buttons keep Blizzard's look.
- **Tooltip & unit frames**: tooltip at the mouse, hidden in combat, class colours, the unit's target, health bar, item and spell IDs; class coloured health bars, combat text on the portraits.
- **Chat & messages**: arrow keys walk through sent messages (kept over a reload), clickable links, short channel names, hidden buttons, input box on top, more lines, copy window; red error messages filtered.
- **Automation** (off by default, Shift pauses): accept and turn in quests separately, with the kinds of quests to accept and the reward choice; gossip, duels, guild invites, group invites from friends, resurrections, "Take all" for mail, "Learn all" at trainers.
- **Camera, sound & travel**: flight time bar with a countdown (measured or estimated routes) and the flight time in the flight map tooltips, `/sundry flights` to export measured routes; larger camera distance, screenshot on level up, muted sounds, `/rl`.
- Options in the game's settings window, minimap button and addon compartment entry, own settings per character, `/sundry inspect`, English and German.
